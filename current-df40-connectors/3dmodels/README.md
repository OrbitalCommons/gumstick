# Component 3D models

All 50 purchased component placements use project-local STEP files. AE1 is an
etched antenna, SJ1 is a copper solder jumper, and H1 is a bare mounting hole;
those PCB features intentionally have no separate component body.

- Standard passive/IC/crystal package models: KiCad packages3d, installed KiCad
  library. See KiCad-LICENSE.md and https://gitlab.com/kicad/libraries/kicad-packages3D.
  These are generic package models, not manufacturer-certified part geometry.
- DF40C_2_0_40DS.step: exact Hirose DF40C(2.0)-40DS manufacturer model,
  downloaded from https://www.hirose.com/en/product/p/CL0684-4042-5-51.
  Source document 0001218396S. Axes normalized to X = connector length,
  Y = width, Z = height, centered on the footprint with seating plane Z=0.
  Normalized envelope: 10.6 x 3.38 x 1.95 mm. Manufacturer ownership retained.
- FC-B1010RGBT-HG_approx.step: locally generated 1 x 1 x 0.9 mm package
  approximation, based on the Nationstar package catalogue:
  https://www.nationstar.com/Uploads/download/20160216094424.pdf.
  Simplified terminals/body; no optical die or wire-bond detail.
- XFL3012_approx.step: locally generated 3 x 3 x 1.3 mm maximum-envelope
  approximation based on Coilcraft's drawing:
  https://www.coilcraft.com/getmedia/f76a3c9b-4fff-4397-8028-ef8e043eb200/xfl3012.pdf.
  Manufacturer CAD was not retrieved. Body and terminals are simplified.

The two approximate models are for visualization/envelope checking, not tooling
or precision mating design. Their generator is tools/hardware/build_package_models.py
at the repository root (CadQuery). Per-reference provenance is in
../docs/3d-models.json. Copper, pads, component positions and routing were unchanged.

DF40C_40DP.step is the exact Hirose 40-contact plug model used by J2,
normalized to a centered XY origin and Z=0 at its solder plane. Download:
https://www.hirose.com/en/product/document?clcode=CL0684-4013-7-51&productname=DF40C-40DP-0.4V(51)&series=DF40&documenttype=3DDrawing_STEP&lang=en&documentid=0001218414S

TP1 and TP2 are etched probe pads, so they intentionally have no body model.

Connector display finishes: `color_connector_models.py` applies gold to the
thin repeated contact faces, silver to plug mounting tabs, and dark gray to
housings. These are visualization assignments on unchanged manufacturer CAD
surfaces, not a new mechanical model or a manufacturer material declaration.

The ACAG0201-2450 STEP is generated by `tools/hardware/build_antenna_model.py` from the same nominal 2 × 1.25 × 0.6 mm envelope as the antenna handoff VRML. It is illustrative, not manufacturer CAD. R63 uses the project-local KiCad 0402 resistor STEP.

The antenna STEP includes the small gray square shown in Abracon's top-view drawing (2025-09-20 datasheet, p.4), toward terminal 1. Mark dimensions/position are approximated from the illustration; the 0.004 mm raised patch is a rendering aid, not a specified marking thickness.
